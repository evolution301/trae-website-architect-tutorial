Frequently Asked Questions

What is the MOQ for custom fabric orders from Fursone? +

Our Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) for custom yarn-dyed or textured fabrics typically starts from 1000 meters per color. For in-stock items in our sample library, we offer flexible minimums starting from 100 meters for sampling.

Can Fursone replicate European luxury fabrics? +

Yes. We specialize in analyzing and reproducing high-end European tweed and knit structures. We can match weight, texture, and composition while offering competitive pricing.

Does Fursone export textiles to international markets? +

Yes. Fursone is an established global textile exporter shipping to fashion brands in North America, Europe, and Asia. We comply with international testing standards (Oeko-Tex, REACH) and handle export logistics.

What is the production lead time for bulk fabric orders? +

Production lead times depend on yarn availability and order volume. Standard bulk production typically takes 25-35 days after lab dip approval. Rush options are available for urgent fashion seasons.

Can I visit Fursone's mill? +

Yes. We welcome fashion designers and buyers to visit our facility to see our production lines and explore our extensive fabric library. Please contact us to schedule a factory tour.

Do you offer fabric swatches for sampling? +

Yes, we provide A4 swatches or sample yardage for designers to test hand-feel and drape. Contact our sales team to request our latest collection books.

What testing standards do your fabrics meet? +

All Fursone fabrics undergo rigorous internal testing for shrinkage, colorfastness, and pilling. We can also provide third-party test reports (SGS, Intertek) upon request to meet specific market requirements.

Do you offer sustainable or eco-friendly options? +

Absolutely. We offer a range of sustainable fabrics using recycled polyester (GRS certified), organic cotton, and eco-friendly dyeing processes to support sustainable fashion initiatives.
