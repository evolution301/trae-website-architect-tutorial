Frequently Asked Questions About Fabric Sourcing from China

Get the Answers You Need

What is the lead time for custom manufacturing?

Custom development takes 7-10 days for sampling and 25-35 days for bulk production from our Wenzhou textile factory. Lead time depends on yarn complexity and order volume.

Do you offer shipping services?

Yes. We handle DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) shipping to the USA, Europe, and Asia. All import duties and logistics are managed by us for a seamless experience.

Can I combine in-stock and custom orders?

Yes. Many clients use our in-stock fabrics for core collections and develop custom fabrics for seasonal hero pieces, balancing speed with exclusivity.

What is the difference between your MOQs?

In-stock sourcing starts at 100 meters (ready to ship). Custom manufacturing requires 1,000 meters per color to ensure yarn stability and color consistency across the batch.

Can I get samples before placing an order?

Yes. Free A4 fabric samples available for evaluation. Custom developments include lab dips and detailed test reports before production.

How do you ensure quality consistency?

We implement a 4-stage QC process: raw material inspection, in-process quality check, finished fabric testing, and pre-shipment verification. Every batch is tested.
