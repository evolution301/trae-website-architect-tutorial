Common Questions About Fabric Sourcing from China

Get the Answers You Need

How does Fursone support small fashion brands?

We support emerging brands with high-end fabric alternatives starting at 100 meters for in-stock items. This allows designers to access luxury-grade textures for prototypes without industrial order minimums.

Do you offer exclusive design rights for wholesalers?

Yes. For qualified partners, we develop exclusive patterns protected for your brand or territory. Our specialty yarn R&D makes us the ideal partner for unique collections.

Why is the custom MOQ 1,000 meters?

To ensure stability of specialty yarns like bouclé and slub, a 1,000-meter threshold is required for custom developments. This ensures intricate textures remain uniform throughout the entire production run.

Can garment factories rely on consistent re-order quality?

Yes. We maintain detailed archives for every custom development. Re-orders achieve 95%+ consistency in color, texture, and hand feel across batches.
